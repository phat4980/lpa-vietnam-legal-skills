# Contract Review

## Review order

1. Identify parties and transaction.
2. Summarize economic obligations.
3. Map key dates and dependencies.
4. Identify one-sided provisions.
5. Check mandatory-law-sensitive clauses.
6. Check internal consistency.
7. Identify operational gaps.
8. Recommend edits.
9. Assign risk severity.

## Risk levels

- CRITICAL — could create severe legal/financial exposure.
- HIGH — material exposure or significant imbalance.
- MEDIUM — meaningful issue that should be negotiated.
- LOW — drafting/clarity issue.
- INFO — observation without material risk.

## Clause rewrite

When proposing wording:

1. Preserve the business objective.
2. Reduce ambiguity.
3. Avoid adding unsupported legal claims.
4. State assumptions.
5. Provide a negotiation-friendly alternative where useful.

Never silently rewrite a commercial term that changes price, credit, liability, or delivery economics.
