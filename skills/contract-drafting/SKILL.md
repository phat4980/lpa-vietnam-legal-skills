# Vietnamese Commercial Contract Drafting

## Drafting sequence

1. Read contract intake.
2. Read company profile/policy when available.
3. Identify transaction-specific legal issues.
4. Verify material legal rules.
5. Build clause architecture.
6. Draft commercially precise language.
7. Run consistency checks.
8. Run legal-risk checks.
9. Run quality gate.
10. Produce final draft plus unresolved items.

## Default B2B goods-sale architecture

Use only clauses relevant to the transaction:

1. Parties
2. Definitions
3. Goods
4. Quality/specifications
5. Quantity/unit
6. Price/VAT
7. Ordering
8. Delivery
9. Inspection/acceptance
10. Non-conforming goods
11. Documents
12. Payment
13. Late payment
14. Rights/obligations
15. Quality claims
16. Penalties
17. Damages
18. Liability
19. Suspension
20. Termination
21. Force majeure
22. Confidentiality
23. Notices
24. Dispute resolution
25. Governing law
26. Term/effectiveness
27. Amendments
28. Signatures
29. Appendices

## Drafting principles

### Precision

Avoid ambiguous terms such as:

- "kịp thời"
- "hợp lý"
- "đầy đủ"
- "sớm nhất có thể"

unless objectively defined.

Prefer measurable conditions.

### Order mechanism

If orders are recurring, define:

- order channel
- minimum notice
- order confirmation
- order modification/cancellation
- cut-off time
- emergency orders
- evidence of order

### Delivery

Define:

- delivery location
- delivery window
- responsibility for unloading
- receiving person
- acceptance document
- shortage/damage process
- risk transfer

### Quality

Define objective acceptance criteria and a practical complaint/replacement process.

Do not promise standards or certifications that the company has not verified.

### Payment

Define:

- invoice basis
- due date
- bank/payment method
- credit terms
- disputed invoice treatment
- late-payment consequences

### Remedies

Check penalty, damages, liability limits, and cumulative remedies against applicable law and the commercial bargain.

Do not automatically copy a generic penalty clause into every contract.

## Final draft rule

A final contract must not contain:

- fake legal citations
- unresolved `{{...}}` placeholders
- contradictory payment terms
- contradictory delivery dates
- undefined abbreviations
- accidental duplicate obligations
