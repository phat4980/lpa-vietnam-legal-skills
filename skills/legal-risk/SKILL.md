# Legal Risk Analysis

## Risk model

Evaluate:

`Probability × Impact × Detectability/Control`

Use qualitative ratings unless quantitative inputs are reliable.

## Risk categories

### Legal
- invalid/unlawful provision
- unenforceability concern
- authority/signature
- regulatory exposure
- limitation periods
- evidence problem

### Financial
- non-payment
- excessive credit
- uncapped damages
- penalty exposure
- tax/invoice exposure

### Operational
- delivery failure
- supply interruption
- quality dispute
- unclear acceptance
- unclear order cancellation

### Relationship
- exclusivity
- unilateral termination
- renewal
- customer dependency
- supplier dependency

### Evidence
- phone/Zalo order without confirmation
- missing delivery records
- missing acceptance records
- inconsistent invoices/documents
