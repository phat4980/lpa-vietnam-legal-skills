# Evidence Policy

## Authority

Legal propositions must be supported by authoritative sources whenever possible.

## Source record

```yaml
source:
  title: ""
  number: ""
  article: ""
  clause: ""
  point: ""
  status: ""
  issued_date: ""
  effective_date: ""
  source_url: ""
  retrieved_at: ""
```

## Verification states

- `VERIFIED` — exact authority and current applicability checked.
- `PARTIAL` — source found but applicability/status incomplete.
- `UNVERIFIED` — not checked against authoritative source.

Only VERIFIED claims may be stated without an uncertainty marker.

## Prohibited behavior

Never fabricate citations or quote legal text from memory.
