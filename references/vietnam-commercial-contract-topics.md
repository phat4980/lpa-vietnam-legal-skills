# Vietnamese Commercial Contract Topics

Use this as a checklist, not as a substitute for current-law research.

## Common topics

- contract formation
- authority and representation
- goods and specifications
- quantity
- price
- taxes/invoices
- order confirmation
- delivery
- inspection
- acceptance
- transfer of risk
- quality claims
- warranty
- payment
- late payment
- suspension
- penalty
- damages
- liability limitation
- force majeure
- termination
- confidentiality
- assignment
- dispute resolution
- governing law

## Research trigger

Whenever a clause materially affects legal liability, do targeted current-law verification instead of relying on this checklist.
