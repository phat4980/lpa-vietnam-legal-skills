# Vietnamese Legal Source Hierarchy

## Tier 1 — Authoritative

Prefer official sources such as:

- national legal-document database
- official gazette
- National Assembly
- Government
- ministries/agencies
- official consolidated legal texts

## Tier 2 — Structured retrieval aids

Examples:

- Vietnamese legal datasets
- legal RAG repositories
- legal knowledge graphs
- indexed legal corpora

These are useful for discovery, retrieval, chunking, metadata, and relationship analysis.

They do not replace the authoritative text.

## Tier 3 — Secondary commentary

- law firms
- legal publications
- professional commentary
- explanatory articles

Use to understand interpretation or practice, then verify material propositions against primary authority.
