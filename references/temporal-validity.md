# Temporal Validity

Vietnamese legal analysis must consider time.

For every material rule, compare:

```text
document issue date
→ effective date
→ amendment history
→ repeal/replacement
→ transitional rules
→ transaction/event date
```

## Questions

1. What law is currently in force?
2. What law was in force when the contract/event occurred?
3. Was the provision amended before the relevant event?
4. Does a transitional provision preserve the old rule?
5. Is the current consolidated text reliable for the relevant historical date?

Do not use today's law automatically for historical disputes.
